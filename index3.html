Straight answers

Still unsure? Send us a WhatsApp message and ask anything.

When should I start?

Ideally 8 to 12 months before the intake. Many scholarships close early, so the sooner we talk, the more options you have.

Can you guarantee admission or a visa?

No. Universities and embassies make those decisions, and anyone who promises otherwise is not being honest with you. What we promise is a complete, carefully prepared application.

I have Form Four or Form Six results. Can I still go?

Yes. Many universities accept students through a foundation year or a diploma. Send us your results and we will show you the routes that fit.

I live outside Dar es Salaam. Can you help me?

Yes. Most of our work happens on WhatsApp and email, so we help students anywhere in Tanzania and East Africa.

How much do your services cost?

It depends on the country and how much support you need. We explain every fee clearly before you commit to anything.
